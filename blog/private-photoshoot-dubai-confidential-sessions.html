Private Photoshoots in Dubai:
Sessions That Stay Between Us

Written by Hatim
Published November 2026
Read time 6 min

A private photoshoot in Dubai is a session made for you alone. The images are never posted, never shown behind the scenes and never mentioned anywhere, and no one but you and your photographer ever sees them. I am a Dubai photographer, and I plan, shoot, retouch and hand over every private session myself, so your pictures travel between the two of us and nowhere else.

What a private photoshoot in Dubai is

Most photoshoots have a second life. The pictures are posted, tagged, added to a portfolio or published. A private shoot has none of that. It exists for one reason: you wanted it.

Clients book private sessions for their own purposes. A fashion editorial they will never publish. Portraits for a gallery wall at home, or for a canvas of their own. A trial of a style they have not tried before. Or simply the experience of being photographed well. None of these needs an audience. Think of Jack drawing Rose in Titanic: a portrait made for one person, and never meant for anyone else.

This is different from a personal branding photoshoot, where the images are made to be used in public, on a website or a profile. A private session is the opposite: the images are never used.

Private fashion, portrait, boudoir and artistic photography

I work in four styles: fashion, portraits, boudoir (intimate, private portraiture) and artistic work. Each gets the same standard of direction, light and retouching. Boudoir and artistic sessions ask for the most trust, which is why the privacy rules below are not optional extras. They are how every private session runs.

How I protect your privacy during a private photoshoot

Six commitments apply to every private session, whatever the style, the outfits or the concept.

Nothing is posted. Not on this website, social media, Pinterest or in any portfolio.

No behind the scenes. No stories, clips or snapshots of the session.

No mention. I do not reference the session or the client anywhere.

A closed set. Once the shoot begins, no one enters, including studio staff and helpers. The only exceptions are chaperones of your choosing.

Images stay between us. The files go from me to you. No third person sees them.

Personal use only. The images are yours for your own use, and they are never used by me.

More than 35 clients have chosen me specifically for this. Many of them ask that their pictures are never posted, and I treat that as the standard, not a special request.

How a private photoshoot in Dubai works, start to finish

I handle every step myself, so there is one person to talk to and one person responsible.

1. Your brief and references. Send me what you have: photos, a Pinterest board, a mood, a style. We discuss the look, the outfits, what you are comfortable with and what you are not.

2. The location. I book the studio or the hotel. A studio gives full control of light and a quiet, closed space. A hotel gives atmosphere and a more personal setting. If you are unsure, this comparison of indoor, studio and outdoor shoots helps.

3. The shoot. The door closes and we begin. I direct throughout, we work at your pace, and you can pause or stop whenever you want. If you would like a chaperone with you, they are welcome to stay.

4. Post-production. I retouch every image myself: natural skin, consistent color, no over-processing.

5. The handover. I check the final files before they leave me, so what you receive matches what we agreed, with nothing missing and nothing extra. Images are delivered only through my own private server link, never through public file-sharing services.

I keep a secure backup of your final images, so that if you ever need them again I can provide them. The backup is never shared or used.

Why one person for everything matters

Every extra person involved is another place a private image can travel. When one photographer books the location, shoots, retouches and delivers, that number stays at one. It also makes the experience simpler for you: you bring the idea, and I take care of everything else.

How to prepare

You do not need experience in front of a camera. What helps is clarity.

References. Collect three to ten images that show the mood, the light and the styling you like.

Outfits. Decide them in advance. What to wear for a photoshoot in Dubai covers fabric, color and fit.

Posing. If it worries you, how to pose for a portrait photoshoot explains how direction works, and I guide you through it anyway.

Boundaries. Tell me before the day. They are respected without discussion.

A chaperone. If you would like one, tell me when you book so the plan includes them.

Frequently asked questions

No. Private sessions are not posted, shown behind the scenes or mentioned, on any platform.

Do you arrange the studio or hotel?

Yes. I book and prepare the location, so you only need to arrive.

Which styles do you shoot privately?

Yes. Boudoir, meaning intimate and private portraiture, is one of the four styles I shoot, and it follows the same rules as every private session: nothing is posted, the set is closed, and you decide what you are comfortable with. A chaperone is welcome.
