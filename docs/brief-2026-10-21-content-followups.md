## 3. Suggested FAQ additions (verified policies only)

All answers below restate wording already on the sites. No new fees, commissions,
review times or contractual promises.

**Models (apply page / models FAQ)**
- *Is there a fee to apply?* No. Applying is free, and paid photography is never a condition of consideration.
- *Who can apply?* Models aged 18 or over who currently live in the UAE.
- *Does applying guarantee acceptance or bookings?* No.
- *Is representation exclusive?* No, it is initially non-exclusive.
- *Does Photoczaro provide visa sponsorship?* No.
- *How will I hear from you?* Official communication comes only through verified Photoczaro channels; never send money in response to unsolicited messages.

**Clients (book-talent page / models FAQ)**
- *Does submitting an enquiry confirm a booking?* No. Availability is checked first.
- *When are usage and fees agreed?* Final usage and fees are agreed before anything is confirmed.
- *Can I enquire about several models at once?* Yes: shortlist them and send one enquiry.
