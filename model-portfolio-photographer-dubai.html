Common Questions

Before you reach out

Do I need modeling experience to book a portfolio shoot?
No. Direction on posing, expression and movement is provided throughout, whether it's your first shoot or a portfolio refresh for an established model.
What's included in a model portfolio session?
A mix of clean digitals, portrait looks, and editorial-style images that show range rather than one repeated look, shaped around what you actually need the images for: agency submission, a personal portfolio, or a comp card.
How is this different from a digitals or comp card shoot?
Digitals and comp cards are usually a subset of a full portfolio session; simple, unedited images built to a specific agency spec. A portfolio session is broader and includes styled, retouched images meant to show your full range. See the full breakdown in the digitals vs. portfolio guide linked above.
How much does a model portfolio shoot cost in Dubai?
Pricing depends on scope: number of looks, locations, and whether comp card or digital design is included. Message me with what you need the images for and I'll come back with a package that fits.
Can you shoot for agency submission requirements?
Yes. If you're submitting to a specific agency, tell me their spec beforehand and I'll deliver a matching set alongside your full portfolio images.
