About

A curated roster, run plainly.

Photoczaro Models presents a curated, non-exclusive roster of UAE-based models to commercial clients, coordinating interest and availability, negotiating booking fees and usage rights, administering the booking agreement, invoicing the client, and remitting compensation to the model.

What Photoczaro Models does

  • Presents selected models to commercial clients.
  • Coordinates model interest and availability.
  • Negotiates booking fees.
  • Negotiates image and video usage rights.
  • Administers client booking agreements.
  • Invoices clients and collects payments.
  • Remits the agreed compensation to models.

What Photoczaro Models does not do

  • Does not employ models.
  • Does not sponsor visas.
  • Does not represent visiting or travelling models.
  • Does not represent social-media influencers or creators.
  • Does not represent anyone under 18.
  • Does not guarantee modelling work.
  • Does not operate a modelling academy.
  • Does not require models to purchase photography services.

Relationship to Photoczaro Photography

Photoczaro Models is part of the Photoczaro family, alongside Photoczaro's editorial and commercial photography practice. The two serve different purposes: the photography practice shoots; the roster represents and books talent. Working with one does not require working with the other.
