Application Terms

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Version: 1.2

These Application Terms explain what happens when you apply to the Photoczaro Models roster. They are separate from the Booking Terms, which apply to clients booking talent.

A request for consideration

Submitting an application is a request for consideration. It does not create a representation agreement, and it does not guarantee acceptance, bookings or income.

Applying is free

There is no fee to apply. Paid photography, courses or any other purchase is never a condition of consideration. Never send money in response to unsolicited messages.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 and currently reside in the UAE. We confirm your age and residency with you during onboarding.

Review

Applications are reviewed privately by Photoczaro. We’ll contact you using the details provided if we would like to proceed. Submitting an application does not guarantee a reply.

Representation

If you are accepted, representation is initially non-exclusive. Detailed representation, payment and booking arrangements are confirmed separately during onboarding.

Bookings

Photoczaro coordinates proposed bookings between clients and models. You decide whether to accept each individual booking. Applying, or choosing work interests, is not consent to any project, wardrobe or image use.

Your public profile

Your application and photos stay private. A public profile is published only after you are accepted and you separately approve it during onboarding.

Your information

We use the details and photos you submit to review your application and to contact you, as described in the Privacy Policy. Do not send passports, Emirates ID or banking details with your application, and do not upload agency contracts. Marketing emails are optional: if you tick the separate marketing box, Photoczaro may email you occasional news and opportunities. It is never a condition of applying, and you can withdraw it at any time by emailing info@photoczaro.com.

Contact

To ask about, update or withdraw an application, email info@photoczaro.com.
