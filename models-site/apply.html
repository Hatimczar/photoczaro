Apply to the Roster

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must currently reside in the UAE.
  • Applying does not guarantee acceptance or bookings.
  • Representation is initially non-exclusive.
  • Photoczaro does not provide visa sponsorship.
  • Photoczaro does not charge a mandatory fee to apply, and paid photography is never a condition of consideration.
  • Never send money in response to unsolicited messages. Official communication only comes through verified Photoczaro channels.

Do not include passports, Emirates ID or banking details in this form. Those are only ever collected later, through a separate secure process, if you're added to the roster.
