Apply to the Roster

Photoczaro Models connects UAE-based models with brands, agencies and production teams. Clients can discover and shortlist talent, while Photoczaro coordinates proposed bookings. You choose whether to accept each opportunity.

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must currently reside in the UAE.
  • Applying does not guarantee acceptance or bookings.
  • Representation is initially non-exclusive.
  • Photoczaro does not provide visa sponsorship.
  • There is no fee to apply. Paid photography is never a condition of consideration.
  • Beware of impersonation: never send money in response to unsolicited messages. Official communication only comes through verified Photoczaro channels.

How it works

  1. Apply — share your details and photographs.
  2. Personal review — Photoczaro reviews your application.
  3. Onboarding — if accepted, confirm the roster arrangements and approve your public profile.

We’ll contact you using the details provided if we would like to proceed.
