Booking Terms

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · Version: 1.0 · Governing law: United Arab Emirates

These Booking Terms govern talent enquiries and bookings administered by Hatim Champeli under the Photoczaro Models brand.

Operator: Hatim Champeli
Brand: Photoczaro Models
Location: Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Email: info@photoczaro.com
Website: models.photoczaro.com

These Booking Terms should be read together with the applicable quotation, Booking Confirmation and Privacy Policy.

1. Definitions

In these Booking Terms:

  • "Photoczaro", "we", "us" or "our" means Hatim Champeli operating under the Photoczaro Models brand.
  • "Client" means the person or organisation requesting or confirming a Booking.
  • "Talent" means an adult UAE-resident model presented or booked through Photoczaro.
  • "Booking" means a Talent engagement confirmed in writing by Photoczaro.
  • "Booking Confirmation" means the job-specific document or electronic confirmation describing the services, fees, dates and Usage.
  • "Production" means the photography shoot, film, video, fashion presentation, e-commerce production, event or other agreed project.
  • "Material" means photographs, video, recordings, artwork or other content featuring the Talent.
  • "Usage" means the authorised commercial or editorial use of Material featuring the Talent.
  • "End Client" means the brand, advertiser or other ultimate beneficiary of a Booking where the Client acts as an agency or production company.

2. Photoczaro's Role

Photoczaro operates a curated, non-exclusive booking roster for adult UAE-resident models.

Photoczaro may:

  • Present selected Talent to prospective Clients.
  • Coordinate interest and availability.
  • Negotiate booking fees and Usage rights.
  • Issue quotations and Booking Confirmations.
  • Enter into Booking agreements with Clients.
  • Invoice and collect payment from Clients.
  • Remit agreed compensation to Talent.

Photoczaro does not employ Talent, sponsor visas, guarantee work, operate a modelling academy or represent visiting models, minors or social-media influencers.

Every Talent remains free to accept or decline a proposed Booking.

3. Enquiries and Booking Confirmation

Submitting an enquiry, requesting availability, receiving a quotation or adding Talent to a shortlist does not confirm a Booking.

A Booking becomes binding only after:

  1. The Talent accepts the proposed assignment.
  2. The Client accepts the Booking Confirmation.
  3. Photoczaro receives the required advance payment in cleared funds.
  4. Photoczaro confirms the Booking in writing.

Photoczaro may withdraw a quotation or provisional hold before confirmation.

If a Booking Confirmation conflicts with these Booking Terms, the Booking Confirmation will take priority for that Booking.

The individual making the Booking confirms that they have authority to bind the Client.

4. Client Information

The Client must provide complete and accurate information, including:

  • Client, production-company and End Client identities.
  • Production date, call time, expected finish time and location.
  • Production type and campaign description.
  • Wardrobe, styling and content requirements.
  • Required skills, movement or performance.
  • Photography, video and behind-the-scenes requirements.
  • Intended media, territory and Usage duration.
  • Paid advertising and outdoor Usage.
  • Product-category exclusivity.
  • Budget and billing contact.
  • Potentially sensitive or elevated-risk requirements.

A material change may require renewed Talent consent, additional fees or a revised Booking Confirmation.

5. Availability and Holds

Talent availability remains provisional until Photoczaro confirms the Booking.

Photoczaro may place a first or second option on a Talent's availability. A provisional hold does not reserve the Talent indefinitely.

If another Client requests the same date, Photoczaro may require the first Client to confirm or release its option within a stated period.

Photoczaro may release a hold where the Client does not respond, accept the Booking Confirmation or pay the required advance by the deadline.

6. Fees

The quotation or Booking Confirmation may include:

  • Talent session or appearance fee.
  • Usage fee.
  • Photoczaro booking or service fee.
  • Overtime.
  • Fittings and rehearsals.
  • Approved transport or other expenses.
  • Applicable VAT.

Quoted fees cover only the services and Usage expressly described. No additional rights are implied.

A shorter Production than originally booked does not automatically reduce the fee because the Talent's agreed time was reserved.

7. Advance Payment and Final Balance

A quotation is valid for seven calendar days unless it states otherwise.

Unless otherwise agreed:

  • An advance payment equal to 50% of the estimated Booking fee is required to confirm the Booking.
  • The advance must be received within 48 hours of acceptance.
  • The remaining balance must be received no later than three business days before the Booking.
  • Bookings confirmed fewer than five business days before the start date require full payment immediately.

Established corporate Clients may receive payment terms of up to 15 calendar days after invoice only where Photoczaro approves those terms in writing and receives a valid purchase order where requested.

Overtime, approved expenses and other amounts that could not reasonably be calculated before the Production are payable within seven calendar days after invoice.

Banking and transfer charges must be paid by the Client so that Photoczaro receives the complete invoiced amount.

8. Usage Conditional on Payment

Commercial Usage rights begin only after Photoczaro has received all applicable Talent, Usage and booking fees in full.

Until payment is complete, the Client may review Material internally but may not publish, distribute, advertise or otherwise commercially exploit it.

Late payment does not extend the authorised Usage period.

Photoczaro may suspend unexercised Usage rights while an undisputed invoice remains overdue, subject to applicable law.

9. Cancellation

The Client must cancel a Booking in writing.

Unless the Booking Confirmation states otherwise, the following charges apply:

  • More than seven business days before the confirmed call time: 25% of the total Booking fee.
  • Between three and seven business days: 50%.
  • Between one and two business days: 75%.
  • Fewer than one business day: 100%.
  • No-show, cancellation after the Talent begins travelling, or cancellation after the confirmed call time: 100%.

Approved and non-recoverable expenses remain payable in addition to the cancellation charge.

The advance payment will be applied against the cancellation charge. Any refundable excess will normally be returned within 10 business days. If the charge exceeds the advance, the remaining balance is payable within seven calendar days.

These charges are intended to compensate Photoczaro and the Talent for reserved availability, administration and lost booking opportunities. They are not intended to operate as a penalty.

10. Postponement

One postponement may be permitted where:

  • Written notice is received at least three business days in advance.
  • The replacement date is within 30 calendar days.
  • The same Talent is available.
  • The Production, Client and Usage remain materially unchanged.

Any remaining credit may be transferred to the replacement date.

A second postponement, an unavailable replacement date or a material change to the Production may be treated as cancellation.

11. Weather

Weather, unsuitable natural light and outdoor-location availability are Production risks unless a separate weather arrangement is stated in the Booking Confirmation.

A weather postponement is subject to the ordinary postponement and cancellation provisions.

If the Talent has begun travelling or arrived at the location, the full session fee remains payable.

12. Working Time and Overtime

The Booking Confirmation should state:

  • Call time.
  • Expected finish time.
  • Included working hours.
  • Breaks.
  • Fittings or rehearsals.
  • Overtime rate.

The booked period begins at the confirmed call time, including where Production is delayed for reasons outside the Talent's control.

The Client must receive approval from Photoczaro and the Talent before extending the Booking. Overtime is charged at the rate stated in the Booking Confirmation and may be rounded up to the next commenced hour.

A delayed start does not oblige Talent to remain beyond the confirmed finish time.

13. Meals, Breaks and Facilities

The Client must provide:

  • Reasonable rest and meal breaks.
  • Drinking water and suitable refreshments.
  • Safe, clean and private changing facilities.
  • Reasonable access to restroom facilities.
  • Appropriate accommodation for dietary or accessibility requirements disclosed in advance.

Public toilets are not considered suitable changing facilities.

14. Expenses and Transport

The Client is responsible for transport, parking, accommodation, meals and other expenses approved in the quotation or Booking Confirmation.

Expenses incurred by Photoczaro or the Talent at the Client's request will be charged at cost unless an administration charge was agreed beforehand.

Photoczaro represents UAE-resident Talent only. International travel and visiting-model bookings are outside the ordinary scope of the roster.

15. Usage Rights

The Client receives only the Usage rights stated in the Booking Confirmation.

The Usage description should identify:

  • Brand and campaign.
  • Media and platforms.
  • Organic or paid distribution.
  • Territory.
  • Duration.
  • Start date.
  • Product category.
  • Exclusivity.
  • Archival rights.

Any additional use requires prior written approval and may require an additional fee.

Unauthorised use includes:

  • Use after the agreed period.
  • Use in additional territories or media.
  • Use by another brand or legal entity.
  • Paid advertising where only organic use was agreed.
  • Use for another product or campaign.
  • Use implying an endorsement not approved by the Talent.
  • Use in defamatory, unlawful, adult or materially undisclosed content.
  • Material alteration creating a misleading or harmful context.

Expired digital Usage must be removed within a reasonable technical period. No new publication, distribution or paid promotion may begin after expiry.

16. Copyright and Likeness Rights

The Talent Booking grants agreed rights relating to the Talent's appearance and likeness. It does not automatically transfer copyright in photographs, video or other Material.

The Client must obtain the necessary copyright or production rights from the photographer, filmmaker or other relevant rights holder.

Where the Client is acting for an End Client, the Client must ensure that the End Client and all production partners observe the agreed Usage restrictions.

17. Artificial Intelligence

Unless separately authorised in writing by Photoczaro and the Talent, the Client must not:

  • Use Talent Material to train or evaluate an artificial-intelligence system.
  • Place Talent Material in a training dataset.
  • Clone or simulate the Talent's face, body, voice or performance.
  • Create a digital replica of the Talent.
  • Generate materially new poses, expressions, speech or actions.
  • Use automated alteration to create a misleading or harmful representation.
  • License Talent Material to an AI or synthetic-media provider.

Ordinary colour correction, cropping and agreed commercial retouching remain permitted within the approved creative context.

18. Social Media

A Booking does not include publishing, promoting or endorsing content through the Talent's personal social-media accounts.

The Client must not require the Talent to post, tag, review or endorse a product unless separately agreed in writing.

Photoczaro does not represent social-media influencers or visiting advertisers.

19. Sensitive Content and Wardrobe

The Client must disclose in advance any requirement involving:

  • Swimwear, lingerie or sheer wardrobe.
  • Intimate positioning or physical contact.
  • Simulated medical, violent or distressing scenarios.
  • Dangerous or strenuous activity.
  • Politically, religiously or culturally sensitive content.
  • Material alteration of appearance.
  • Nudity or implied nudity.

Talent may decline undisclosed or materially changed requirements without penalty.

Consent to one wardrobe, pose or context does not imply consent to another.

20. Safety and Conduct

The Client is responsible for providing a lawful, professional and safe working environment.

The Client must:

  • Use competent production personnel.
  • Maintain suitable supervision.
  • Prevent harassment, intimidation and discrimination.
  • Obtain required location and production permissions.
  • Maintain safe equipment and facilities.
  • Disclose elevated risks.
  • Prevent unauthorised photography or recording.
  • Respect the Talent's agreed personal boundaries.

Talent may pause or leave a Production that is unsafe, abusive or materially different from the confirmed brief.

Where the Client's breach causes the Production to stop, the agreed fee may remain payable.

21. Insurance

Photoczaro does not provide production, venue, equipment, travel, medical, personal-accident or cancellation insurance for the Client.

The Client is responsible for maintaining insurance appropriate to its Production, including where reasonably applicable:

  • Public liability insurance.
  • Production and equipment insurance.
  • Employer or crew insurance.
  • Personal-accident coverage for higher-risk Talent activity.
  • Cancellation insurance.

The Client must disclose activities involving vehicles, animals, water, heights, stunts, pyrotechnics, hazardous equipment, extreme conditions or another elevated risk.

Photoczaro or the Talent may request reasonable evidence of insurance before accepting a higher-risk Booking.

Nothing in this section excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded under UAE law.

22. Talent Unavailability

If confirmed Talent becomes unavailable through illness, emergency or another circumstance outside reasonable control, Photoczaro will use reasonable efforts to:

  • Offer suitable replacement Talent.
  • Reschedule the Booking.
  • Refund amounts received for Talent services that cannot be performed.

Photoczaro does not guarantee that an identical replacement will be available.

Photoczaro is not responsible for the Client's separate production expenditure solely because of genuine Talent unavailability, except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

23. Future Bookings and Direct Contact

For six months after Photoczaro introduces or books a Talent for a Client, subsequent professional bookings of that Talent by the Client, its End Client or an associated production party should be arranged through Photoczaro.

The Client must not use contact information obtained during a Booking to bypass Photoczaro's booking services.

This does not prevent ordinary professional communication during a confirmed Production.

24. Confidentiality

The Client and Photoczaro must protect confidential information received in connection with a Booking, including:

  • Unreleased campaigns and products.
  • Commercial rates.
  • Personal contact information.
  • Production materials.
  • Private Talent information.

Confidential information may be disclosed to professional advisers, insurers or authorities where reasonably necessary and lawfully permitted.

25. Complaints

Concerns relating to conduct, safety, payment or Usage should be reported promptly to: info@photoczaro.com

Performance-related concerns should normally be reported within two business days so the circumstances can be investigated effectively. This does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

Photoczaro will review complaints reasonably and in good faith.

26. Force Majeure

Neither party will be responsible for delay or failure caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including severe weather, government restrictions, venue closure, transport disruption, public emergency, widespread technical failure or serious illness.

The affected party must notify the other promptly. The parties will first attempt to reschedule or reasonably modify the Booking.

Any refund or cancellation charge will take account of services already performed and non-recoverable expenses.

27. Liability

Photoczaro will administer Bookings with reasonable care.

To the extent permitted by UAE law:

  • Photoczaro is not responsible for indirect or consequential loss.
  • Photoczaro does not guarantee a particular creative or commercial result.
  • Photoczaro's aggregate liability relating to a Booking will not exceed the fees paid to Photoczaro for that Booking.

Nothing excludes or limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

28. Consumer Rights

Nothing in these Booking Terms excludes mandatory rights available under UAE consumer-protection law.

Where the Client acts as a consumer, a provision that conflicts with a mandatory consumer right applies only to the extent legally permitted.

29. Electronic Acceptance

Quotations, Booking Confirmations, acceptances and notices may be exchanged electronically.

Acceptance may be recorded through email, an authorised online process, electronic signature or another reliable method demonstrating agreement.

30. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

These Booking Terms and every Booking are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the Emirate of Dubai.

The courts of Dubai will have jurisdiction unless mandatory law requires another forum.

Before commencing formal proceedings, the parties should first attempt to resolve the dispute through good-faith written discussions.

31. Changes to These Terms

Photoczaro may update these Booking Terms to reflect operational, legal or regulatory changes.

The version accepted when a Booking is confirmed will continue to govern that Booking unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
