Booking Terms

Development note: this is placeholder draft text, not professionally approved legal copy. It must be reviewed by UAE-qualified legal counsel before this site or any booking agreement goes live.

1. Role of Photoczaro

Photoczaro acts as booking representative for models on its roster. Photoczaro is not the model's employer and does not sponsor visas or guarantee work.

2. Enquiries and quotations

Submitting a booking enquiry does not confirm a booking. Model availability is verified after enquiry, and Photoczaro will return a quotation reflecting the production, usage and duration requested.

3. Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only once Photoczaro and the client agree the fee, usage rights and schedule in writing, and any deposit required is received.

4. Usage rights

Usage rights (channel, territory, duration, exclusivity) are negotiated per booking and apply only to what is agreed in writing. Any use beyond the agreed scope requires a separate agreement and fee.

5. Cancellations and rescheduling

Cancellation and rescheduling terms, including any applicable fees, will be set out in the individual booking agreement.

6. Payment

Photoczaro invoices the client directly and remits the agreed compensation to the model after payment is received, per the individual booking agreement.

7. Conduct on set

Clients are expected to provide a safe, professional working environment consistent with UAE law and industry standard practice.
