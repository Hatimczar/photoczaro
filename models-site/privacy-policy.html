Privacy Policy

Development note: this is placeholder draft text, not professionally approved legal copy. It must be reviewed by UAE-qualified legal counsel (including UAE data-protection law) before this site goes live.

1. What we collect

From clients: name, company, contact details and production details submitted via the Book Talent form. From applicants: professional and legal name, contact details, physical statistics, languages, skills, digitals/headshots, and an optional portfolio link, submitted via the Apply to the Roster form.

2. What we don't collect through public forms

We do not request passports, Emirates ID, visa documents, banking information, exact personal schedules or dates of birth through the public application form. Those are only ever collected later, through a separate secure process, for applicants who are added to the roster.

3. How we use it

Client data is used to verify model availability and issue a quotation. Applicant data is used to assess roster fit and, where a model is added to the roster, to present that model to prospective clients using only their professional name unless separate written permission covers their legal name.

4. Storage and access

Submissions are stored on infrastructure operated for Photoczaro and accessible only to authorized Photoczaro staff. Uploaded files are not stored in a public directory.

5. Model profile data shown publicly

Public model profiles show only professional information the model has agreed to publish: professional name, category, general UAE location, height, measurements, hair/eye colour, languages, skills and portfolio images. Phone numbers, personal email addresses, ID documents, exact addresses and dates of birth are never published.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us at the address on the Contact page.

7. Third parties

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared with clients only to the extent needed to confirm a specific booking they have enquired about.
