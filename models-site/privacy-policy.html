Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Version: 1.2 · Governing law: United Arab Emirates

This Privacy Policy explains how Photoczaro Models collects, uses, stores and shares personal data through models.photoczaro.com and in connection with roster applications, Client enquiries and Talent bookings.

1. Who We Are

Photoczaro Models is operated by Hatim Champeli under the Photoczaro brand from Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

For the purposes of Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 Concerning the Protection of Personal Data, Hatim Champeli determines how and why personal data covered by this Policy is processed.

Data Controller: Hatim Champeli
Brand: Photoczaro Models
Location: Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Email: info@photoczaro.com
Website: models.photoczaro.com

Photoczaro Models and Photoczaro Photography are related services under the Photoczaro brand. Personal data is not automatically transferred between them unless necessary, disclosed in this Policy or authorised by the individual concerned.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to models.photoczaro.com.
  • Clients and prospective Clients.
  • Individuals applying to the roster.
  • Talent accepted onto the roster.
  • People communicating with Photoczaro Models.
  • Individuals whose information is included in a legitimate Production or booking enquiry.

Separate agreements, releases and privacy terms may apply where Photoczaro Photography creates photographs or video.

3. Personal Data We Collect

Website visitors

We may process:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested pages.
  • Approximate location derived from network information.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Security and diagnostic information.
  • Referral information.

Client enquiries and bookings

We may collect:

  • Name.
  • Company and job title.
  • Work email and telephone number.
  • Billing information.
  • Campaign or project details.
  • Production dates and location.
  • Budget.
  • Talent shortlist.
  • Wardrobe and content descriptions.
  • Usage channels, territories, duration and exclusivity.
  • Contracts and booking confirmations.
  • Communications, invoices and payment records.

Roster applicants

We may collect:

  • Professional and legal name.
  • Email and telephone number.
  • UAE city.
  • Confirmation that the applicant is at least 18.
  • Confirmation of UAE residency.
  • Height and relevant measurements.
  • Hair and eye colour.
  • Languages and professional skills.
  • Experience level.
  • Portfolio or social-media links voluntarily supplied.
  • Headshots, full-length digitals and portfolio materials.
  • Application statements and communications.
  • Consent and application records.

The public application form does not request passports, Emirates ID documents, banking details or visa documents.

If an applicant joins the roster, additional information may later be collected through a separate secure process where necessary for booking administration, verification, payment or legal compliance.

Accepted Talent

For accepted Talent, we may additionally process:

  • Public professional profile information.
  • Approved portfolio and digital images.
  • Availability and booking preferences.
  • Booking acceptance and refusal records.
  • Rates and negotiated fees.
  • Usage restrictions.
  • Contracts and consent records.
  • Payment information.
  • Residency or work-status verification.
  • Emergency contact information where appropriate.
  • Complaints, safety concerns or incident records.

Photographs are personal data where a person can be identified. Photoczaro does not use roster photographs for facial recognition or biometric identification.

4. How We Collect Information

We collect personal data:

  • Directly from you.
  • Through website forms.
  • During application and onboarding.
  • During booking negotiations.
  • Through email, telephone or authorised messaging services.
  • From Clients where necessary for a proposed Booking.
  • From Talent where necessary for a proposed Booking.
  • From Photoczaro Photography where an appropriate transfer has been authorised.
  • Automatically through hosting, security and essential website technology.

We may verify professional or publicly available information where reasonably necessary to prevent fraud or confirm an applicant's identity, age, residency or professional experience.

5. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:

  • Review roster applications.
  • Communicate with applicants.
  • Create and manage approved Talent profiles.
  • Present Talent to prospective Clients.
  • Build booking decks and shortlists.
  • Coordinate Talent interest and availability.
  • Prepare quotations and Booking Confirmations.
  • Negotiate fees and Usage.
  • Administer contracts, invoices and payments.
  • Maintain consent and Usage records.
  • Handle complaints and safety concerns.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain and improve the website.
  • Comply with UAE legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

Photoczaro does not sell or rent personal data.

Applicant and Client information will not be used for unrelated third-party marketing.

If you tick the optional marketing box on the application form, Photoczaro may email you occasional news and opportunities about Photoczaro Models. This consent is separate from your application, is never a condition of applying, and is not ticked by default. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing info@photoczaro.com. Photoczaro records when consent was given.

Photoczaro does not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Lawful Processing

Photoczaro processes personal data only where permitted under applicable UAE law.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on:

  • Your clear consent.
  • Steps you request before entering into an agreement.
  • Performance of a roster, Booking or service agreement.
  • Compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation.
  • Protection and enforcement of legal rights.
  • Another lawful basis recognised under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law.

Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent for future processing.

Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully and may not require deletion where another lawful retention obligation applies.

7. Talent Profiles and Image Consent

A Talent profile will be published only after the individual joins the roster and authorises the relevant information and images.

The public profile may contain:

  • Professional name.
  • UAE location.
  • Height and agreed measurements.
  • Hair and eye colour.
  • Languages.
  • Professional skills.
  • Approved portfolio and digital images.
  • Category or new-face status.

Photoczaro will not publicly display:

  • Legal names without permission.
  • Private email addresses or telephone numbers.
  • Emirates ID or passport information.
  • Banking information.
  • Residential addresses.
  • Exact schedules.
  • Private contractual rates.

Permission to display an image on the roster does not grant a Client commercial Usage. Commercial Usage requires a confirmed Booking and agreed licence.

Talent may request reasonable correction of public profile information by contacting info@photoczaro.com.

8. Shortlists

The website allows visitors to create a shortlist of Talent.

The shortlist is initially stored in the visitor's browser. Photoczaro does not receive it until the visitor submits a booking enquiry or intentionally shares it.

The shortlist should expire automatically after 90 days. It may be removed earlier by clearing the shortlist or deleting browser storage.

Shortlists are not used for behavioural advertising.

9. Sharing Personal Data

Photoczaro shares personal data only where reasonably necessary.

Clients and production companies

Approved public profile information and booking-relevant information may be shared with prospective or confirmed Clients.

Private identification, banking and personal contact information will not be shared unless necessary, lawful and appropriately disclosed.

Talent

Relevant Client, brand and Production information may be shared with Talent so they can make an informed decision about a proposed Booking.

Service providers

Personal data may be processed by selected providers supporting:

  • Website hosting and security.
  • Form processing.
  • Email and communications.
  • Private file storage.
  • Accounting and invoicing.
  • Payment processing.
  • Electronic signatures.
  • Technical and administrative support.

Service providers may process information only for the relevant service and subject to appropriate security and confidentiality requirements.

Professional advisers

Information may be shared with lawyers, accountants, insurers and professional advisers who require it to provide their services.

Authorities and legal proceedings

Photoczaro may disclose information where required by UAE law, a court order, a competent regulatory authority or a lawful enforcement request.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

10. International Transfers

Some hosting, communications, storage or business-service providers may process information outside the UAE.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, Photoczaro will use measures required by the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, which may include:

  • Providers in jurisdictions offering appropriate protection.
  • Contractual and organisational safeguards.
  • Data minimisation.
  • Appropriate consent where required.
  • Another lawful transfer mechanism.

Submitting a form is not treated as unlimited consent to transfer personal data for unrelated purposes.

11. Retention Schedule

Photoczaro retains personal data only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable UAE obligations.

Incomplete applications

Incomplete or abandoned application uploads will normally be deleted within 30 days.

Rejected or withdrawn applications

Unsuccessful or withdrawn applications will normally be deleted or anonymised within six months after the decision.

Information may be retained longer where required for a complaint, safety matter or legal claim.

Future-opportunity talent pool

An applicant may separately agree to remain in a future-opportunity talent pool for up to 12 months.

Talent-pool consent is optional and is not a condition of applying.

Accepted Talent

Roster information will be retained during the active relationship.

A public profile will normally be removed within 10 business days after the roster relationship ends, subject to active Bookings, existing Usage and legal obligations.

Non-financial roster-administration records will normally be deleted or anonymised within 24 months after the relationship ends.

Client enquiries

An enquiry that does not result in a Booking will normally be deleted within 12 months after the last meaningful communication.

Booking and financial records

Booking agreements, invoices, payment records and supporting transaction documents will be retained for at least seven years following the relevant tax period, or longer where required by UAE law, an audit or an active dispute.

Consent and Usage records

Image consent, release and Usage records will be retained for the duration of the authorised Usage and normally for seven years afterward to document the permission granted.

Complaints and safety incidents

Complaint, safeguarding and incident records will normally be retained for five years after closure.

They may be retained longer where an investigation, dispute or legal claim remains active.

Technical logs

Routine website and security logs will normally be retained for no more than 90 days unless needed to investigate fraud, abuse or a security incident.

Backups

Information deleted from active systems may remain in restricted rolling backups for up to an additional 90 days before being overwritten.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted, securely anonymised or placed under restricted access where continued retention is legally necessary.

12. Your Rights

Subject to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and applicable limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about personal data held about you.
  • Obtain access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where no lawful reason for continued processing applies.
  • Request restriction or cessation of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Obtain or transfer certain information where legally and technically applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for future processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Raise a complaint about how your information is handled.

Requests should be sent to: info@photoczaro.com

Photoczaro may request reasonable proof of identity before acting on a request.

We will respond within the period required under applicable UAE law.

Information may be retained despite a request where necessary for contracts, payments, tax obligations, legal proceedings or another lawful purpose.

13. Applicants Under 18

Photoczaro Models accepts applications only from people aged 18 or over.

Photoczaro does not knowingly represent minors or invite them to submit roster applications.

If Photoczaro discovers that a person under 18 has submitted an application, processing will stop and the information will be deleted unless limited retention is required for safeguarding or another legal obligation.

Concerns about information submitted by a minor should be reported to info@photoczaro.com.

14. Cookies and Similar Technology

The website uses limited technical storage and security technologies.

These may include:

  • Browser storage used for the shortlist.
  • Essential security technology provided by the website-hosting or content-delivery provider.
  • Language, accessibility or session preferences where implemented.

Photoczaro Models does not currently use the shortlist for advertising or behavioural profiling.

With your consent, Photoczaro Models uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (for example which pages are viewed and from which country). Google Analytics sets cookies and sends usage data, including a shortened IP address, to Google. It is not loaded unless you choose Accept on the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Declining stops measurement and clears the Google Analytics cookies. Analytics is not used on the admin panel and is not used for advertising or behavioural profiling.

If other non-essential analytics, marketing cookies or advertising technologies are introduced, this Policy and the website's consent controls will be updated before activation.

Visitors can clear cookies and browser storage through their browser settings. This may remove saved shortlist and preference information.

15. Security

Photoczaro uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption.
  • Access-controlled storage.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Secure form transmission.
  • File-size and file-type restrictions.
  • Spam and abuse controls.
  • Separation of public profiles from private applications.
  • Restricted access to contracts and payment information.
  • Periodic review of access permissions.

Application files must be stored privately and not placed in a publicly accessible website directory.

No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Where a personal-data breach requires notification under UAE law, Photoczaro will notify the competent authority and affected individuals as required.

16. Relationship With Photoczaro Photography

Photoczaro Photography and Photoczaro Models serve different purposes.

A model is not required to purchase photography services to apply to or remain on the roster.

Where Photoczaro Photography creates images, its photography agreement, release and privacy terms may also apply.

Personal data or photographs collected through Photoczaro Photography will not be added to the Models roster without appropriate notice and authorisation.

17. Website Content and Intellectual Property

The website's roster layout, text, branding and original materials are protected by applicable intellectual-property law.

Portfolio images remain the property of their respective copyright holders and are displayed under permission or licence.

Visitors may not scrape, reproduce, republish, create datasets from or commercially exploit roster content without written authorisation.

Accessing a model profile does not grant commercial rights in the Talent's likeness or portfolio images.

18. Changes to This Policy

Photoczaro may update this Privacy Policy when its services, technology, providers or legal obligations change.

The revised effective date will appear at the beginning of the Policy.

Where a material change significantly affects previously collected information or existing consent, Photoczaro will provide additional notice or obtain new consent where required.

19. Contact and Complaints

Questions, concerns, complaints and data-rights requests should be sent to:

Photoczaro Models
Operated by Hatim Champeli
Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Email: info@photoczaro.com
Website: models.photoczaro.com

Photoczaro will investigate privacy concerns reasonably and in good faith.

If you believe your rights under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law have been infringed and the matter has not been resolved, you may submit a complaint to the competent UAE data-protection authority in accordance with its available procedures.
