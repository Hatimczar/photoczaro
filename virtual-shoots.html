What It Is

Professional Direction, Wherever You Are

A Virtual Shoot is a remotely directed portrait session with Photoczaro. Before the session, we align on the visual direction, styling, available space and lighting. During the shoot, you receive live guidance on camera position, framing, pose and expression so the final images remain intentional and editorial.

The exact setup depends on the device and space available to you. Photoczaro reviews the setup before the booking is confirmed so expectations remain clear.

Preparation

What You Need

  • A compatible phone or camera
  • A reliable internet connection
  • A separate device for the live call where required
  • Natural window light or a controllable light source
  • Enough space to move and adjust the camera
  • Agreed wardrobe, styling and props
  • Time for a short technical check before the session

Professional equipment is not automatically required. The available setup is reviewed before booking so the creative direction can be adapted realistically.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a professional camera?
Professional equipment is not automatically required. The phone, camera, light and space you have available are reviewed before the booking is confirmed, so the creative direction can be adapted realistically to your setup.
How is the photoshoot directed remotely?
Photoczaro provides live direction on framing, pose, expression, light and camera placement through the agreed connection method, after the concept and setup have been planned in advance.
Can I book from outside the UAE?
Virtual Shoots are available internationally, subject to Photoczaro reviewing your device, internet connection, available lighting, space and intended concept.
What happens if my lighting or internet is not suitable?
The setup is reviewed before the booking is confirmed so major limitations can be identified early. Not every space or connection will be a fit, and that is addressed during the planning stage rather than during the session.
Are the final images professionally edited?
Selected images are processed and delivered as part of the confirmed session package. Exact retouching scope is agreed as part of that package.
Can modelling agencies book Virtual Shoots?
Agencies may enquire about remote test shoots for individual talent. Suitability is assessed according to the model's location, available setup and intended image use.
How are the images delivered?
Delivery method and timing are confirmed as part of the session agreement, once the concept and package have been discussed.
How do I book a session?
Use the enquiry form on this page or message Photoczaro directly on WhatsApp with your location, preferred dates and the purpose of the shoot.
