Preparation

  • Identify the purpose of the images before the shoot
  • Share visual references for the concept
  • Plan outfits in advance
  • Arrive groomed and ready for the session
  • Arrive on time
  • Bring backup wardrobe options where appropriate
  • Avoid drastic last-minute styling changes
  • Tell Photoczaro about any comfort boundaries in advance
  • Ask questions before the shoot begins

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need modelling or photoshoot experience?
No. Photoczaro works with clients at every stage, from a first-ever photoshoot to experienced models refreshing a portfolio. Direction is provided throughout the session.
Will you help me with posing?
Yes. Active guidance on posing, expression and movement is part of every session, whether it is your first shoot or your tenth.
What should I wear for the shoot?
Wardrobe is discussed as part of planning the direction before the booking is confirmed, based on the concept and intended use of the images.
Can we shoot in a studio or outdoors?
Both are possible. The right setting depends on the concept, and is agreed as part of confirming the session.
How many final images will I receive?
Final image count depends on the confirmed package. This is agreed before the session so expectations are clear.
How long does editing and delivery take?
Turnaround depends on the agreed package. Delivery timing is confirmed as part of the booking.
Do you provide RAW or unedited images?
RAW or unedited files are not included by default. Any exception must be agreed before the booking is confirmed.
Do you offer TFP shoots?
Selectively. Photoczaro occasionally considers TFP or creative collaborations where the concept and intended outcome align with the portfolio. TFP is not guaranteed and is not a substitute for a client booking.
Can you help me build a modelling portfolio?
Yes. Model portfolio and test-shoot sessions are designed to show range, expression, movement and camera presence for submissions and resubmissions.
Can agencies book through this page?
This page is built for individual clients and models booking directly. Agencies booking roster shoots or scouting sessions should visit the dedicated For Agencies page.
Can I use the images commercially?
Image usage is defined in the booking agreement. Commercial use requires explicit approval and may require a separate licence or quotation.
How do I confirm my booking?
A booking is confirmed only once the scope, price, date and terms have been agreed between you and Photoczaro.
