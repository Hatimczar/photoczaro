Common Questions

Before you reach out

Do you work with models who have no professional experience?
Yes. I work with models at every stage, from complete beginners doing their first-ever shoot to experienced models refreshing their portfolio for agency resubmission.
Do you offer test shoots (TFP) for models building a portfolio?
I take on test shoot collaborations selectively, usually with models who have a clear direction for the kind of portfolio they need. Message me on WhatsApp with a couple of reference photos and what you're going for, and I'll let you know if it's a fit.
Can agencies submit models directly for scouting shoots?
Yes. I work directly with Dubai modeling and talent agencies on scouting shoots, agency submission packages, and ongoing roster photography. Message me on WhatsApp and we can set up a call to discuss volume and turnaround.
How fast can I get a shoot booked?
For most portfolio and portfolio-refresh sessions, I can usually schedule within a few days depending on my calendar. WhatsApp is the fastest way to check current availability.
What languages do you work in?
I regularly work with English, Russian, French, Spanish and Czech-speaking models and agencies, and the site itself is available in all five languages.
